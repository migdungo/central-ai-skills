## What Is Central AI?

**Central** is an AI-powered Business Operating System (BOS) built for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). Its core product is an **AI Voice Receptionist and AI Chat Assistant** that handles incoming calls, books appointments, captures leads, and manages customer communications — all automatically, 24/7.

Central's tagline positions it as a way for busy business owners to never miss a call or lead again, without hiring additional staff.

**Website:** https://trycentral.com
**Support:** https://help.trycentral.com

---

## Core Products & Features

### 1. AI Voice Receptionist (Phone)
- Answers calls 24/7 — including after-hours, weekends, and overflow calls
- Books appointments live during the call by connecting directly to the business's calendar
- Checks availability in real time and avoids scheduling conflicts
- Sends instant email and calendar confirmations to both the business and the customer
- Speaks **30+ languages** for multilingual customer support
- Customizable voice: choose from various accents, genders, and tones to match your brand

### 2. AI Chat Assistant (Website Widget)
- Embeds as a customizable chat widget on any website
- Handles customer inquiries, lead capture, and appointment booking via chat
- Available at trycentral.com/chat and trycentral.com/live-chat-ai-software

### 3. Unified Inbox
- Aggregates all customer communications into one shared dashboard
- Channels supported: phone calls, website chat, WhatsApp, email, SMS, and social media
- Team members can reply, assign, and manage conversations from a single view

### 4. Free Built-in CRM
- Automatically captures caller details, notes, and follow-up actions
- Organizes all lead and customer data in one place
- No separate CRM subscription required at entry level

### 5. Business Intelligence & Analytics
- Tracks the most common customer questions
- Shows real-time resolution rates and live analytics
- Surfaces conversation intent signals (e.g., how many calls come from serious buyers vs. browsers)
- Helps businesses identify patterns and improve every customer interaction

### 6. Human + AI Hybrid Option
- Central offers an optional pairing of AI agents with trained human colleagues from **Wing Assistant**
- Gives businesses automation reliability combined with human warmth for edge cases

## Target Audience

Central is built for **small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs)** across multiple verticals:

- **Home Services / Contractors** — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, cleaning
- **Retail Stores** — physical and e-commerce
- **Professional Services** — any service business relying on inbound calls and appointments
- **Any SMB owner** who is too busy to answer every call or can't afford a full-time receptionist

**Core pain points Central solves:**
- Missing calls after hours or during busy periods
- Losing leads to competitors who pick up the phone
- Spending too much time on repetitive admin (scheduling, FAQs, follow-ups)
- High cost of hiring a full-time human receptionist (~$35,000–$50,000/year vs. $1,100–$4,800/year for Central)

## Sales & Customer Success Notes

### For Sales Conversations:
- Lead with **cost vs. hiring**: Central at ~$90–$400/mo vs. a receptionist at $3,000–$4,000/mo
- Emphasize **missed call = missed revenue** — especially powerful for home services and retail
- The **10-day free trial** is a strong close — low commitment to start
- Ask prospects: *"How many calls do you miss per week after hours?"* — then quantify lost revenue
- Zapier integration means Central can plug into almost any existing workflow

### For Customer Success:
- Onboarding key steps: connect calendar, configure AI voice persona, set up call routing rules
- Watch for customers who haven't connected their calendar — they won't get full appointment booking value
- Analytics dashboard is a key retention tool — help customers find their win stories in the data
- Expansion opportunity: upsell the human hybrid (Wing Assistant) add-on for high-stakes use cases
- Common churn risk: customer feels the AI sounds "too robotic" → guide them to customize voice/tone settings
